### 16 July 2025

- Implement Data Collection + **FTI (Feature, Training, Inference)** architecture in any LLM app. Also building an LLM Twin — reading the *LLM Engineer's Handbook*.
- Started reading *The Intelligent Investor*.
- **Diagram trick:** use an LLM to generate mermaid code, debug it in the [live editor](https://mermaid.live/), then in [draw.io](https://app.diagrams.net/) use Arrange → Insert → Mermaid. This gives you a starter for complex flow diagrams. Very useful.
