### 26 August 2026

- **Vectorize.** Cloudflare provides a [vectordb solution](https://developers.cloudflare.com/vectorize/get-started/intro/) as well, with a generous free tier. Would be good to add this into my website chatbot solution.

### 27 August 2026

- **Lighthouse.** Lighthouse is an open-source, automated tool to improve the quality of web pages. It has audits for performance, accessibility and SEO. Can be integrated with CI/CD before any new release.
- **Lychee.** [Lychee link checker](https://github.com/lycheeverse/lychee) is a good fit for content-driven websites. Broken links can be found and fixed at the CI level itself.

### 29 August 2026

- **Guardrails.** Implementing guardrails in AI applications at times significantly increases the app's latency.
- **Prompt injection.** SFT used to defend LLMs against prompt-injection attacks often fails by relying on surface-level patterns rather than understanding malicious intent. Good read: [arxiv.org/pdf/2601.07185](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.07185)
- **LLM gateways.** Explored [tensorzero](https://github.com/tensorzero/tensorzero) while exploring open-source LLM gateway options. Another option worth exploring for enterprise: [Portkey gateway](https://github.com/Portkey-AI/gateway). TrueFoundry, Kong are some others.
- **Caching.** Caching, when not implemented properly, can cause data leaks. Important to consider in the AI application world.

### 30 August 2026

- **Web scraping.** [Scrapling](https://github.com/D4Vinci/Scrapling) is a web scraping framework with anti-bot and Cloudflare bypass capability.
- **Chatbot feedback.** User feedback extracted from chatbot conversations can be used for evaluation and personalization of the application.
