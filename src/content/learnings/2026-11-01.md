### 27 October 2026

- fck-nat is an open-source project https://fck-nat.dev/stable/ whose name stands for “Feeling Cheap, Kind-of NAT”. It’s a tiny EC2 instance that does the same job as an AWS-managed NAT Gateway, but for a fraction of the cost.

### 28 October 2026

- A complex system that works is invariably found to have evolved from a simple system that works. The inverse proposition also appears to be true: A complex system designed from scratch never works and cannot be made to work. John Gall, Systemantics (1975)
- If you can afford to sometimes lose messages, you can probably get higher throughput and lower latency on the same hardware.
