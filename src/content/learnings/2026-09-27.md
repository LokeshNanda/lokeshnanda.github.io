### 24 September 2026

- **Agent efficiency.** Microsoft's fastcontext project reports that in GPT-5.4 trajectories, reading and searching accounts for 56.2% of all tool use turns and 46.5% of main agent total tokens. Considering this, the largest efficiency gain in a coding agent is not a better model or better diff format, it is a smarter retrieval.
